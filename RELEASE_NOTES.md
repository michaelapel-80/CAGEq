A steadier oscilloscope.

- **Changed:** the detached oscilloscope draws fast-moving parts of the trace the way a real beam
  would, with its own Speed dimming setting (the main window's is unchanged).
- **Changed:** the scope instruments now get audio in evenly spaced 20 ms windows instead of
  uneven ones, so they move more evenly.
- **Fixed:** the oscilloscope flickered on a steady signal.
- **Fixed:** the oscilloscope's peak lines jumped on a steady signal such as a square wave.
