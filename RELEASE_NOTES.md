A detachable oscilloscope, and smoother spectrum motion on ordinary displays.

- **New:** the oscilloscope can be opened in its own resizable window, like the vectorscope. It
  keeps its own L/R or mixdown choice there, starting on L/R.
- **Changed:** the spectrum analyzer's trace moves as smoothly on a 60 Hz display as on a 240 Hz
  one: fast changes leave a continuous smear instead of separate lines.
- **Changed:** in a detached window, or on a maximised main window, the scope beams keep the width
  they have inline instead of growing with the screen.
- **Fixed:** stopping a test tone froze the instruments for a moment, and could click.
- **Fixed:** the level meter's true peak could read a little high at the start of playback and
  after every pause.
